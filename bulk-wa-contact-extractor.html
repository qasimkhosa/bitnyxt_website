How to export WhatsApp contacts to Excel

  1. Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open WhatsApp Web and sign in. Refresh the page if it was open during installation.
  3. Click the extension toolbar icon to open the sidebar.
  4. Choose Groups, Chat List or Business Labels and select the sources you need.
  5. Preview the available rows and warnings, then download the XLSX workbook.

Free to install. Trial leaves the first 10 rows visible and masks later rows with ****. Check your available data before purchasing Pro.

Why are phone numbers missing?

Only information made available by WhatsApp Web can be exported. Hidden or unavailable numbers are not guaranteed to be recoverable. An upgrade does not guarantee missing data will become available.

Can I use the workbook with my CRM?

Check your CRM's accepted formats and field mapping. If it requires CSV, open the relevant worksheet in your spreadsheet application and save that sheet as CSV.

Need help with the sidebar?

Refresh WhatsApp Web, wait for chats to load, and click the extension icon again. Contact support with your extension version and error text if the problem continues. Do not post private contact lists or license keys publicly.
